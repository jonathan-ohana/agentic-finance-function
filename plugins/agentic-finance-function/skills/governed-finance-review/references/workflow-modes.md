# Workflow modes

## Close review

- Reconcile the general ledger to the approved control total.
- Identify missing evidence, duplicate or unusual postings, cut-off issues, accrual gaps, balance-sheet reconciliations, and source-versus-ledger contradictions.
- Separate proposed adjustments from carried exceptions.
- Do not describe the close as complete until the approved blocking checklist is satisfied or the finance owner explicitly accepts the residual risk.

## Variance analysis

- Confirm the comparator and plan vintage before calculating variance.
- Keep amount, percentage, sign, polarity, and materiality distinct.
- Build a price, volume, mix, timing, or other driver bridge only where inputs support it.
- Commentary should state what moved, the supported driver, what remains unattributed, and the decision or owner question.
- Do not extrapolate a single month into a reforecast recommendation unless the relevant playbook or evidence supports it.

## Management reporting

- Lead with the verdict and decisions required.
- Use a small scorecard selected for the current decision, not every computable KPI.
- Preserve adverse caveats on the same surface as the metric they qualify.
- Put check detail and lineage behind the main read without removing it from the artifact.
- Use neutral presentation for on-track items so attention remains available for exceptions.

## Reforecast

- State the actual-through period and forecast start.
- Separate assumptions, measurable anchors, scenarios, and outputs.
- Use one scenario selection point rather than independent toggles that can disagree.
- Keep capital requirements separate from operating cash; do not silently plug financing.
- State horizon and resolution limits. A thirteen-week cash view should not imply a long-term runway conclusion.

## Finance-function assessment

- Map each failure to one of four causes: mapping, export, source system, or operating process.
- Name the consequence before the remediation.
- Prioritize the smallest control that prevents or detects the issue.
- Recommend automation only after the workflow, owner, evidence, and approval boundary are clear.
